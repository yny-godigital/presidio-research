Further to issuance of the LOI, Gamuda M&E Sdn. Bdn. (GME) and Pro-Cahaya Industries
Sdn. Bhd. (PCI) have reached the following mutual understanding on 13 March 2025: -
1. To enter into an agreement for a Sub-Contract Sum of RM148,888,000.00 (Ringgit
Malaysia: One Hundred Forty-Eight Million and Eight Hundred Eighty-Eight
Thousand Only).
2. PCI to provide a Performance Bond for a sum equivalent to 10% of the Sub-Contract
Sum, after omission of Prime Cost (PC) Sum.
3. The 10% Bond value shall be reduced to half at 5%, upon satisfactory completion of
Testing and Commissioning (T&C) works by PCI.
4. PCI to submit payment applications (excluding PC Sum portion) in accordance with
the following payment milestones: -
